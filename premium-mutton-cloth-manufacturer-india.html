What Is Mutton Cloth and Why Is It Important?

Mutton Cloth is a knitted cotton fabric known for softness, flexibility, stretchability and absorbency. Unlike conventional rigid woven materials, knitted fabric can adapt to curved or irregular surfaces.

Industrial users select suitable grades of Mutton Cloth for wiping tools, cleaning equipment, polishing components, absorbing moisture and handling materials during manufacturing operations.
